Use this as a quick checklist before you buy any everyday tee.

## 1. Check GSM

Under 180 GSM often feels thin for oversized cuts. Around **220 GSM** gives premium structure.

## 2. Prefer combed cotton

Smoother yarn, better durability, kinder on skin.

## 3. Ask about dyes

Chemical-safe processes matter for comfort, especially if you wear a tee all day.

## 4. Confirm the fit language

“Oversized” should come with measurements. Use a [size guide](/size-guide).

## 5. Care still matters

Cold wash, shade dry, iron inside out — see our [care guide](/care-guide).
