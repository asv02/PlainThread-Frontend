**GSM** stands for grams per square metre. It measures fabric density — how much fibre sits in each square metre of cloth.

## Is 220 GSM thick?

Yes, relative to thin fashion tees. **220 GSM** is a premium mid-weight: substantial enough to hold an oversized silhouette, still breathable for everyday wear.

## How it feels

You notice it immediately — more body in the hand, cleaner drape on the shoulder, less see-through in lighter colours. After washing, a well-made 220 GSM tee keeps structure instead of going limp.

## Why oversized tees need it

Oversized fits amplify fabric weaknesses. Light GSM can look baggy rather than intentional. Heavier combed cotton helps the tee look designed, not accidental.
