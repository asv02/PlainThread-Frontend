Not all cotton feels the same. The difference often starts in how the fibre is prepared before spinning.

## Carded cotton

Carding cleans and aligns fibres, but shorter strands remain. Those short fibres can create a rougher hand-feel and more pilling over time.

## Combed cotton

Combing takes the process further — removing shorter fibres so the yarn becomes smoother and stronger. That is why **combed cotton** feels softer against skin and wears cleaner after repeated washes.

## Why it matters for skin

If you want a **skin friendly oversized t-shirt**, fibre quality matters as much as dye safety. Fewer rough ends means less irritation and a more premium everyday feel.
