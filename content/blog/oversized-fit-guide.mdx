An oversized tee should feel intentional — relaxed through the chest and shoulder, not accidentally large.

## True to size for the designed drape

If you want the silhouette you see in product photography, start with your regular size. Drop shoulders and extra length are part of the cut.

## Size down for cleaner lines

Prefer less volume? Size down one. You still get comfort, with a slightly closer outline.

## Unisex by design

Plain Thread oversized tees are **unisex**. The same cut works across genders — style it with denim, cargos, or shorts.
