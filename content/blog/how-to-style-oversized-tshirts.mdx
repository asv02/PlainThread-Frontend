Oversized tees are easiest when you treat them as a calm base.

## Monochrome

Black tee + black jeans + clean sneakers. Instantly sharp.

## Street minimal

White oversized tee + cargos + simple sneakers.

## Warm weather

Pink or white tee + tailored shorts. Keep footwear minimal.

## Layering

Wear under an open shirt or overshirt. The denser 220 GSM fabric holds its line under layers.
