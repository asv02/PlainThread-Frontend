Polyester can look sharp and dry quickly. Cotton breathes differently — and for all-day wear in Indian weather, that difference matters.

## Cotton

Natural fibre. Softer hand-feel when combed well. Better airflow for many people across a full day.

## Polyester

Strong and wrinkle-resistant, but often warmer and less pleasant against skin for long wear.

## The Plain Thread choice

We use **100% combed cotton** at 220 GSM — no polyester blend to fake structure. Structure comes from density and cut instead.
