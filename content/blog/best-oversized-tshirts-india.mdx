Looking for the **best oversized t-shirt in India** usually means balancing fabric quality, fit honesty, and everyday comfort — not chasing the loudest print.

## What actually matters

Most people start with colour. Better buyers start with **GSM**, **fibre quality**, and **silhouette**. A tee that looks oversized in photos can still feel thin, clingy, or shapeless after two washes if the fabric is underbuilt.

## Why 220 GSM stands out

Typical fashion tees sit around 160–180 GSM. At **220 GSM**, you get more structure — especially important for oversized cuts with drop shoulders. The fabric drapes instead of collapsing.

## Combed cotton vs cheaper blends

Combed cotton removes short fibres that cause roughness. Polyester blends can look sharp in a listing photo and feel hotter on a full day. If you want a **healthy cotton t-shirt**, start with 100% combed cotton.

## Minimal > graphic-heavy

Graphics date quickly. A **minimal oversized t-shirt** in black, white, or a soft pink works across seasons and outfits — jeans, cargos, shorts, sneakers.
